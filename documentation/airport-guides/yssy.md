# YSSY - Sydney Kingsford Smith International Airport

## About SYD
Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (IATA: SYD, ICAO: YSSY) is the primary international airport serving Sydney, Australia. It sits on Botany Bay, about 9 km (5.6 mi) south of the Sydney central business district, and is the busiest airport in Australia. The field elevation is 21 ft AMSL and the airport has three runways: 07/25, 16L/34R and 16R/34L.

## Airspace
The simulated Sydney terminal area extends to 60 NM with a ceiling of FL280. Arrivals are sequenced onto the MARLN, MEPIL, NASHO and RIVET STARs; departures use the AVMOV, OLSEM, KEVIN, TONTO and BOREE SIDs. The default flow is 16R for departures and 34L for arrivals.

## Procedures
YSSY uses RNAV SIDs and STARs. Instrument departures turn onto their published tracks as modelled in the airport file; arrivals join a STAR, descend via the published restrictions and then expect radar vectors to the assigned runway.

| Flow            | Departure            | Arrival             |
|-----------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Normal (Default)| 16R                  | 34L                 |
| South           | 16L, 07              | 34L, 34R            |
| East            | 16R, 34R             | 07, 25              |

## Positions
Sydney Delivery / Ground, Sydney Tower, Sydney Approach and Sydney Control. The main runways are worked by separate Tower and Approach directions in real operations.
